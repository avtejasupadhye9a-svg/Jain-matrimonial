# Security and privacy

- Auth uses passwordless email OTP with random six-digit codes, HMAC storage, ten-minute expiration, a resend cooldown, limited guesses and keyed rate buckets. Production authentication requires at least 32 characters of AUTH_SECRET and a verified Brevo sender.
- Session tokens have 256 bits of entropy. D1 stores their hashes. Access lifetime is 15 minutes, and rotating refresh credentials have a 30-day absolute expiry. Native clients store refresh tokens in platform secure storage. Logout revokes the session.
- Worker routes check roles and record ownership. The local admin bootstrap requires an explicit local-only flag, a runtime fixture secret, loopback URL, and synthetic identity. Keep all fixture settings out of production. Provision the first admin manually through an approved migration/change and a verified mailbox.
- Discovery and contact release use explicit server-side rules. Set DISCOVERY_DETAILS=false to disable the additional details projection. The preview excludes names, exact birth dates, contact details, employer, private family data, freeform descriptions and moderation data. Unclaimed, suspended, hidden, blocked and unapproved profiles are excluded.
- Customer profile visibility can be withdrawn immediately by hiding the profile. Consent revocation stops future contact lookups. Stored or copied contact information is outside this control. Phones require an independently verified channel before they can be offered. Only verified email sharing is enabled.
- Current candidate age floor is 21 across locales and genders as a conservative product choice. Confirm region-specific launch rules and the full privacy/visibility policy before onboarding real users. See the [Prohibition of Child Marriage Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2055/1/A2007-06.pdf).
- Keep secrets in protected local environment settings or Cloudflare secret storage. Review logs for accidental PII exposure. Audits record identifiers and actions. OTP codes, raw sessions, contact details and private documents are excluded from audit payloads.
- Privacy operators must verify requests, process export or deletion within the agreed policy, and document purge across D1, notification/audit retention, permanent PC files, staging, backup expiry, and legal holds. Define time limits and deletion approvals before public launch.
