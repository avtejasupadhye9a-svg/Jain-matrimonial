# Security and privacy

- Auth uses passwordless email OTP with random six-digit codes, HMAC storage, ten-minute expiration, a resend cooldown, limited guesses and keyed rate buckets. Production authentication requires at least 32 characters of AUTH_SECRET and a verified Brevo sender.
- Session tokens have 256 bits of entropy. D1 stores their hashes and access lasts at most 15 minutes. Customer refresh tokens retain their 30-day absolute expiry and platform secure storage. The Admin app keeps tokens in memory and Admin sessions have an eight-hour absolute limit. Logout revokes the session.
- Admin access requires a session tied to the one configured internal account and current credential version. The dedicated password endpoint limits attempts to six per source IP and thirty account-wide per ten-minute window. Password-change attempts are throttled separately. A password change updates the verifier, revokes every Admin session, and writes the audit event atomically. Customer OTP continues to authenticate customer accounts only.
- The Admin verifier stores a random 32-byte salt and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 output after 100,000 iterations on a peppered HMAC input. The independent 32-byte pepper belongs in Worker secret storage. The iteration count respects the hosted Workers Web Crypto limit. Use a strong unique passphrase of at least 20 characters. [Cloudflare Web Crypto](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/runtime-apis/web-crypto/) · [Workerd iteration-limit discussion](https://github.com/cloudflare/workerd/issues/1346)
- Initial setup runs once through the authenticated D1 operator script. Legacy account IDs and records are preserved. There is no HTTP setup or password recovery route. Keep local customer OTP fixtures disabled in production. An owner who loses the password needs a separately authorized database maintenance procedure.
- The production operator sends the verifier as a bound parameter over HTTPS to the D1 management API. The database rejects malformed verifiers, replacement, deletion, and account reassignment. Passwords and peppers stay in the operator process. Test failure diagnostics omit response bodies that could contain credentials.
- Discovery and contact release use explicit server-side rules. Set DISCOVERY_DETAILS=false to disable the additional details projection. The preview excludes names, exact birth dates, contact details, employer, private family data, freeform descriptions and moderation data. Unclaimed, suspended, hidden, blocked and unapproved profiles are excluded.
- Customer profile visibility can be withdrawn immediately by hiding the profile. Consent revocation stops future contact lookups. Stored or copied contact information is outside this control. Phones require an independently verified channel before they can be offered. Only verified email sharing is enabled.
- Current candidate age floor is 21 across locales and genders as a conservative product choice. Confirm region-specific launch rules and the full privacy/visibility policy before onboarding real users. See the [Prohibition of Child Marriage Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2055/1/A2007-06.pdf).
- Keep secrets in protected local environment settings or Cloudflare secret storage. Review logs for accidental PII exposure. Audits record identifiers and actions. OTP codes, raw sessions, contact details and private documents are excluded from audit payloads.
- Privacy operators must verify requests, process export or deletion within the agreed policy, and document purge across D1, notification/audit retention, permanent PC files, staging, backup expiry, and legal holds. Define time limits and deletion approvals before public launch.
